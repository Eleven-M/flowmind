# Code of Conduct

FlowMind follows the Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and direct.
- Keep technical disagreement focused on evidence, tradeoffs, and reproducible behavior.
- Assume maintainers and contributors are working with limited context.
- Do not post secrets, private logs, customer data, or credentials.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or discriminatory language
- Publishing private information without permission
- Repeated off-topic or disruptive comments
- Pressuring contributors to expose private infrastructure or credentials

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior makes collaboration unsafe or unproductive.

Report conduct issues privately through the repository maintainers or GitHub reporting tools.
